Our Mandate & Structure

Uncompromised Analysis. Zero Commercial Bias.

Conflict of Interest is an independent, non-profit digital publication dedicated to rigorous analysis at the intersection of law, technology, politics, and global economics. In an era where media ecosystems are increasingly captured by corporate sponsorship and algorithmic outrage, we operate on a radically different foundational premise: expertise over clicks.

Our newsroom is uniquely structured as an international collective. Every brief, docket analysis, and essay published on this platform is authored by advanced university students, legal researchers, and credentialed graduates holding degrees directly within the fields they cover. From antitrust law in Washington to regulatory frameworks in Brussels and supply chain economics in East Asia, our contributors write from the frontlines of academic and policy research.

"True objectivity requires structural independence. As a registered non-profit initiative, we answer to neither shareholders nor political action committees—only to the evidentiary standard of our research."

By bridging the gap between dense academic journals and rapid-fire breaking news, Conflict of Interest provides readers with institutional-grade briefings that clarify the hidden mechanics of global power.
